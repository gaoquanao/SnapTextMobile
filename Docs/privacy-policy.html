隐私政策 · Privacy Policy

拾文 SnapText · 生效日期 Effective date: 2026-10-05

简体中文

拾文（以下简称「本 App」）的设计原则是：你的数据只属于你，且只停留在你的设备上。

我们收集什么

什么都不收集。本 App：

  • 没有账号体系，无需注册登录
  • 没有网络请求，App 不向任何服务器发送数据
  • 不包含任何分析、统计、广告或第三方 SDK
  • 不进行任何形式的用户追踪

数据存储在哪里

  • 截图、识别出的文字、标题、标签等全部归档数据，仅存储在你的设备本地（App 沙盒与应用组容器内）
  • 删除 App 即删除全部数据；你也可以随时在 App 内逐条删除
  • 本 App 不提供云同步功能（未来若提供 iCloud 同步，将基于你的个人 iCloud 账户，且会更新本政策）

权限说明

权限用途触发时机
照片读取你选择的截图/照片进行本地文字识别；「相册自动收集」开启后读取新增图片仅在你自己选择图片或开启相应功能时
相机拍照识别与文档扫描仅在你点击拍照/扫描时

儿童隐私

本 App 不收集任何数据，适合所有年龄段使用。

English

SnapText ("the App") is built on one principle: your data belongs to you and stays on your device.

What We Collect

Nothing. The App:

  • Has no accounts and no sign-in
  • Makes no network requests; the App sends no data to any server
  • Contains no analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party SDKs
  • Does not track you in any form

Where Your Data Lives

  • Screenshots, recognized text, titles and tags are stored only locally on your device (inside the app sandbox and app group container)
  • Deleting the App deletes all of its data; you can also delete entries individually at any time
  • The App offers no cloud sync (if iCloud sync is added in the future, it will use your personal iCloud account and this policy will be updated)

Permissions

PermissionPurposeWhen requested

CameraSnap & recognize, document scanningOnly when you tap those buttons

All processing (OCR, keyword/summary extraction) uses on-device system frameworks and never leaves your device.

Exports

When you actively use an export feature (Notes, Obsidian, Bear, custom URL templates, the system share sheet), the content is handed to the destination app or share sheet you choose; the destination's own privacy policy then applies.

Children's Privacy

The App collects no data and is suitable for all ages.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and note it in the App's release notes.
